# Short Drama Apps & Websites Directory (2026)

A complete, regularly updated list of **short drama apps and websites**: 93 platforms for vertical dramas, micro dramas and mini series, with links to each official website, App Store page and Google Play listing.

Short dramas (also called **vertical dramas**, **micro dramas**, **mini dramas**, **reels dramas** or *duanju* 短剧) are serialized shows filmed in portrait mode for phones, with episodes that usually run one to three minutes. Apps like ReelShort, DramaBox, ShortMax and GoodShort made the format popular, and dozens of new platforms launch every year. This directory tracks all of them in one place.

> **Looking for a specific drama?** [Dramadex](https://www.dramadex.com) indexes the full catalog of shows across every platform below, so you can find a title and see where to watch it.

## What is a short drama?

A short drama is a scripted series made for mobile viewing. Each episode is a few minutes long, shot vertically, and ends on a cliffhanger. A full series often runs 50 to 100 episodes and can be watched in a couple of hours. Common genres include romance, revenge, billionaire and CEO stories, werewolf and fantasy, and family drama.

The format started in China and spread worldwide through apps such as ReelShort and DramaBox. Most platforms let you watch the first episodes for free, then unlock the rest with coins, a subscription, or by watching ads.
